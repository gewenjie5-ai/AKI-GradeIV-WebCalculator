What is PNI?

PNI is a composite index derived from serum albumin and peripheral blood lymphocyte count. It is calculated automatically and entered into the frozen prediction equation. Albumin and lymphocyte count are used only to derive PNI and are not additional predictors in the model.

Model information and use boundary

This calculator reproduces the final shrinkage-adjusted prediction equation developed in a single-center retrospective cohort of patients undergoing Grade IV abdominal surgery.

The model estimates the probability of postoperative acute kidney injury within 7 days using information available by the completion of surgery.

For convenience, this webpage derives PNI automatically from preoperative serum albumin and peripheral blood lymphocyte count. These two source values are used only to calculate PNI and are not additional predictors in the frozen 11-predictor model.

The model underwent bootstrap internal validation in the development cohort. Independent external validation has not yet been performed.

For research and independent evaluation only. This calculator does not provide treatment recommendations or define clinical intervention thresholds.

All calculations are performed locally in the browser. Entered values are not transmitted or stored by this webpage.
